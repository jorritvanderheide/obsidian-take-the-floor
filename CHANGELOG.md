# Changelog

What changed in each release of Take the Floor, newest first.

## Unreleased

- **Present** shows a note as slides in a window of its own, with `---` between
  slides, and your speaker notes in a pane in Obsidian. Notes are your
  `%%comments%%`, and they never reach a slide.
- With a second screen, the slideshow fills it and your notes stay in Obsidian. With
  more than two, it asks which. On niri, Windows, macOS and X11 it moves there by
  itself. On other Wayland desktops you drag it, and F makes it fill the screen.
- A screen you connect during a talk gets the slideshow, and so does one that comes back.
- A note with the property `presentation: true` has a Present button in its title bar.
  **Present** also works on any other note, from the ribbon, the file menu or the
  command palette.
- Citations come out as in Due Credit. Each slide has the full reference of what
  it cites at its foot, and the reference list closes the talk.
- While you present, the page and arrow keys and the space bar change slides,
  wherever your cursor is, Escape ends the talk, and your computer won't lock or
  go to sleep.
- The notes pane shows the slide on the screen, the next one and a timer, and says
  when a slide is too long to fit. B, the full stop or the eye button hides the slides,
  and you can then edit your note and watch the slide change in the pane. Esc, the
  eye button or your clicker brings them back, with your changes.
- **Preview slides** opens a tab with every slide of a note, which updates as you
  save, and marks any that are too long.
- **Export slides to PDF** makes a PDF with one slide to a page, without your notes.
  It needs no LaTeX.
- The slides follow your Obsidian theme and CSS snippets.
